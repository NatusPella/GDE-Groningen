The user decided on 2026-10-03 that the game will be called **GDE Groningen – Zomer vol misdaad**, where GDE stands for *Grootste Diefstal Ever*.

- **Series format:** GDE + city or province + a subtitle per game, e.g. GDE Drenthe or GDE Leeuwarden. It must stay franchise-able.
- **Dropped:** the "VI" numeral and the words "Grand Theft".
- **Rejected expansions:** "Grote Diefstal Eierbal" (the user didn't like it), "Gewoon Dieven, Eigenlijk" and others.
- **Rollout:** after the 0.23 merges and the legal agent's conflict check. It covers the logo (own OFL font, no Pricedown look-alike), the title screen, the trailer and name cards, the README, the release file names, the app name and the store texts.
- **Keep the internal `gte-*` keys** so saves keep working.

**Why:** reduce the GTA trademark risk (Steam, publicity) and make sequels possible.

**How to apply:** use the new name in new user-facing text once the rename lands, and never reintroduce "VI" or "Grand Theft". See [[release-and-publishing]].
